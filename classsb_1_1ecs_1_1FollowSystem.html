Detailed Description

Updates the Transform of entities with PositionFollower to match a target's position + offset.

Entities with a PositionFollower component will adopt the position of their specified target entity, adjusted by a fixed offset. This system is ideal for linked movement behaviors such as the ball sticking to the paddle or trailing visual elements.

Requires both Transform and PositionFollower components on the follower entity. Skips followers if the target entity is not valid or lacks a Transform.
