Detailed Description

Processes PendingHit and decrements entity durability, marking for destruction if depleted.

Entities with a PendingHit will have their HitsRequired reduced. If the value reaches zero or below, the entity is marked with a PendingDestroy component, allowing a subsequent DestructionSystem to remove it.

Also supports future extensions like score updates, sound triggers, and visual feedback.

Requires both PendingHit and HitsRequired on the same entity.
